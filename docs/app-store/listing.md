## App name (23/30)

Field Sales: Rep Orders

## App introduction (89/100)

Let field reps visit shops, take orders from your live stock and send them to your store.

## App details (415/500)

Field Sales is for brands that sell to retail shops through a field team. Reps plan visits, check in with GPS at the shop, and take orders against your current stock. Orders go to your store as draft or confirmed orders, with the retailer created as a customer. Managers see visits, orders and sync status, retry failed syncs, and run commission for reps. Reps use a mobile web app; managers work inside your admin.

## Features (8/8, each up to 80)

1. Plan rep visits and record GPS check-in and check-out at each shop (66)
2. Take orders against live stock from the locations you choose (60)
3. Send orders to your store as drafts or confirmed orders (55)
4. Create retailers as store customers automatically (49)
5. See sync failures with the store's message and retry in one click (65)
6. Calculate rep commission by flat %, product, slab or per order (62)
7. Send payment links by email or WhatsApp with Razorpay (53)
8. Manager dashboard and reports on visits, orders and targets (59)
