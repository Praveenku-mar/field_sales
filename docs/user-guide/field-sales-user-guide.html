Field Sales User Guide: Web and Mobile

Nov 17, 2026 · Praveenkumar

Overview

Field Sales lets sales reps visit retail shops with a GPS check-in, take orders against live Shopify stock, and push those orders to Shopify automatically. Managers track visits, orders, sync status and commission from one dashboard.

There are two ways in:

  • Web app: open Field Sales from Apps in your Shopify admin, or open https://<your-site>/fieldsales in a browser. Managers and admins do setup and oversight here. Reps can use it on a phone too.
  • Mobile app: the Field Sales phone app (Android and iOS), built for reps and managers in the field, with offline support and push notifications. It uses the same site, account and data as the web app.

Who does what:

RoleWhat they can do
System Manager (admin)Everything, including the store connection, Sync Failures, Sync Log and all Settings tabs
Sales ManagerSees their whole team. Plans and imports visits, creates reps, manages shop logins and commission, views Reports. Can move a shop's map pin
Sales RepSees only their own shops, visits and orders. Takes orders, checks in and out, sees their own commission
Shop login (Field Sales Retailer)Sees only My Orders, read-only. Can download invoices

The web app calls customers Shops and the mobile app calls them Customers. They are the same records, and each one is pushed to Shopify as a customer.

Getting started

Everyone signs in with the same email and password on the web and on the phone. New reps get a welcome email with a link to set their password. New shop logins get a "Welcome to <store name>" email.

Sign in on the web

  1. Go to https://<your-site>/fieldsales (short form: /fs).
  2. Sign in on the login page. You land back in Field Sales.
  3. Optional, on a phone: open the user menu and tap Install app (or Add to Home Screen) to add a home-screen icon.

Inside the Shopify admin there is no login: open Field Sales from Apps and it loads in place.

Sign in on the mobile app

  1. Install the app and open it.
  2. Tap Configure server (or Change server), enter your site address, for example yourcompany.example.com, and tap Save server. The app adds https:// itself, and the address must not include a path.
  3. Enter your Email or Username and Password, then tap Sign In.
  4. Optional: in Profile → Connected Instance & Security, turn on Screen lock or Biometric unlock to lock the app when it is reopened.

You stay signed in until you tap Log Out or the server ends the session. Signing out clears all offline data on the phone, so sync first (see the mobile app section).

Forgotten passwords and lockouts

  • Five wrong attempts lock the account for 10 minutes. The app shows the same "Invalid login credentials" message for a wrong password, a locked account, or a user with no Field Sales role.
  • Forgot password? in the mobile app only shows a note. Reset from the web login page instead, or ask a manager to click Send Reset Link on your Sales Rep page.

Web app: connect Shopify and sync

Field Sales connects to your store when you install it from the Shopify App Store: Shopify grants the access you approve, and there is no shop address, key or password to enter. After that, products and stock flow in from Shopify, and shops and orders flow out to it. Store options are in Settings → Store Connection.

First-time setup, in order

  1. Install the app. Install Field Sales from the Shopify App Store and approve the requested access. It opens inside your Shopify admin, picks your first location as the default and starts the first product sync.
  2. Check locations. In Settings → Store Connection, click Fetch Locations, tick Sell from here for each location reps sell from, and click Set Default to change the default. Changing the default starts a product sync.
  3. Set the order mode. Under Create Shopify Order As, choose Draft Order (staff review each order in Shopify) or Confirmed Order (orders go straight to Shopify). Also pick a Product Sync Frequency: every 30 minutes, hourly or daily.
  4. Add an email account. In Settings → Email Account, click New Email Account and tick Default outgoing account. Without one, no invites or order confirmations are sent.
  5. Fill in the Invoice tab: company name, GSTIN, address, state, phone and email. These are printed on invoices.
  6. Add sales reps (see the next section).
  7. Plan visits for the reps.

Click Save Changes after editing any tab. Discard undoes unsaved edits.

What syncs, and when

DataDirectionWhen
Products, prices, barcodes, stockShopify → Field SalesOn the sync schedule, plus live updates whenever Shopify changes a product or a stock level
ShopsField Sales → Shopify (as customers)When a shop is saved, or by clicking Sync to Shopify on the shop
OrdersField Sales → ShopifyWhen a rep submits an order
Paid, fulfilled, cancelled and refunded eventsShopify → Field SalesLive; they drive commission

Products are read-only in Field Sales: edit names, prices and stock in Shopify. A manager can click Sync Products on the Products page to pull changes immediately.

Draft orders vs confirmed orders

  • Draft Order: each order arrives in Shopify as a draft (for example #D5). The Shopify order number, commission and the customer's confirmation email follow only after staff complete the draft in Shopify. Field Sales checks for completed drafts every 30 minutes.
  • Confirmed Order: a real Shopify order is created as soon as the rep submits.

Webhooks for live updates are set up by the app. Do not add them by hand under Shopify admin → Settings → Notifications, because those are signed with a different key and will be rejected.

Web app: day-to-day tasks

The sidebar holds every page your role can open: Notifications, Dashboard, Orders, Shops, Products, Visits, Sales Reps, Commission, Reports, Sync Failures, Sync Log and Settings. On a phone, a bottom bar shows Dashboard, Orders, Shops and Visits.

Dashboard

The page is titled Analytics. It shows total sales, orders, completed visits and pending syncs, plus Today's Operations and Exceptions requiring follow-up: missed visits, shops not visited in 30 days, draft orders, pending shops and out-of-stock products.

Shops

  1. Click New Shop. Enter Shop Name, Phone, Owner Name, Shop Type, address and Country (the country sets how the phone number is checked). Add the GSTIN for Indian shops.
  2. Set the location with Use My Location or Select Location on a map. Geofence Radius (m) defaults to 50.
  3. Choose the Sales Rep and save.

On a shop's page you can Edit, Plan Visit Today or start a New Order. If the "Shopify Customer Sync" card shows Draft or Failed, click Sync to Shopify. Only managers can move a map pin after it is set.

Visits

  1. Click New Visit, then choose the shop, rep, date and purpose (Order Taking, Collection, Merchandising, Complaint or Follow-up).
  2. On the visit date, the assigned rep opens the visit and clicks Check In. The browser asks for location access. A check-in more than the geofence radius from the shop is allowed but flagged ("You are N m away from the retailer").
  3. Take an order with New Order, or record why no order was taken.
  4. Click Check Out. Without an order, pick a Reason: Stock Available, Owner Absent, Price Issue, Shop Closed or Other (Other needs details).

A rep can have only one visit in progress at a time. A missed visit is a planned visit whose date has passed; ask a manager to move it to today. Inside the Shopify admin the browser gives no location: click Open in Field Sales on the visit to check in from a new tab, or use the mobile app.

Importing visits (managers): on Visits → Import from Excel, click Download Template and fill one row per visit with the columns Sales Rep, Customer and Visit Date (DD-MM-YYYY), plus Purpose and Notes if needed. Then click Choose File, check the preview and click Create N visits. The limit is 500 rows per file, as .xlsx or .csv. Duplicate rows and visits that already exist are skipped and listed.

Orders

  1. Click New Order, then choose the Shop, Sales Rep, Order Date and Location, and click Create Draft.
  2. Add items by searching, with Scan barcode, or with Add Row or Add Multiple. Set Discount % (capped by the rep's limit) and the tax rate: 0, 5, 12, 18 or 28%.
  3. Click Save, then Submit. Submitting re-checks live Shopify stock. Use Preview to see the printed order.

Order statuses run Draft → Pending → Synced, with Failed if Shopify rejects the order. Cancelled orders keep their record. From More actions you can Refresh Stock, Open in Shopify, Cancel Order (submitted orders) or Delete (drafts). Cancelling in Field Sales does not cancel the order in Shopify, and a paid order must be refunded before it can be cancelled.

Invoices and email: each synced order sends the shop one confirmation email with the invoice PDF attached. On the order page, use Download Invoice or Resend Email. The invoice prints as a GST "Tax Invoice" when a GSTIN is set on the Invoice tab, otherwise as "Invoice". To stop the emails, untick Send order confirmation in Settings → Invoice.

Payments: Field Sales does not take payments. Payments are collected or recorded in Shopify, and Field Sales follows.

  • Online: the shop pays on Shopify's checkout with any payment provider set up in Shopify (Settings → Payments, for example Razorpay or UPI). On a draft order, use Pay in Store Checkout, Copy Link or WhatsApp on the order's Payment card to send the checkout link. When Shopify shows the order paid, Field Sales marks it Paid (for a draft, once it is completed, within 30 minutes).
  • Cash or bank transfer / UPI: open the submitted order, click Mark as Paid, choose the method and, for a transfer, enter the reference number. Then click Mark Paid in Shopify: it opens the order in the Shopify admin, where you use Collect payment → Mark as paid.
  • Orders → Payment → To record in Shopify lists orders paid in Field Sales that Shopify doesn't show as paid yet. An order leaves the list once Shopify shows it paid.
  • An unpaid order has Collect Payment in Shopify, to send an invoice or record a payment there. Payments recorded in Shopify drive commission.

Sales reps (managers)

Click New Sales Rep and fill in User (Email), Full Name, Mobile, Territory, Reports To, Monthly Target and Max Discount % per Order (leave it empty to allow no discount). Pick the role Sales Rep or Sales Manager, tick Email an invite to set a password, and click Create. A rep's page has Send Reset Link and Set Password.

Commission

Reps see their own earnings on My Commission: Earned, Pending, Reversed and Net. Managers also get Team, Statements and Plans. Plans pay a flat %, a rate per product, tiered slabs or an amount per order, counted when the order is Paid or Fulfilled in Shopify. Statements are drafted on the 1st of each month; a manager clicks Approve, then Mark Paid. Companies that don't pay commission can turn it off in Settings → Commission.

Shop logins (My Orders)

A manager can give a shop its own login from the shop's Login Access card: enter an email and click Send Invite. The shop then sees only My Orders, where it can view its orders and payment status, Download Invoice, and pay through Shopify with Pay in Store Checkout when the order has a Shopify checkout link. Use Remove access to revoke the login.

Sync failures (admins)

Sync Failures lists orders and shops that Shopify rejected, each with a Retry button. Failed orders are also retried automatically every 15 minutes, up to 5 times. Sync Log shows every sync attempt and its result.

Plans

You choose your Field Sales plan on Shopify's plan page, and charges appear on your Shopify bill. Settings shows your current plan. If a plan limits the number of reps or orders per month, Field Sales tells you when the limit is reached, with a link to change your plan.

Mobile app

The mobile app covers a rep's whole day: see customers, check in, take an order, check out. It keeps working without a signal. It asks for location access (for check-in), the camera (for barcode scanning) and, optionally, biometrics (for app lock).

Getting around

RoleBottom tabs

Commission appears only when commission is turned on. The side menu adds Products, Notifications, Profile, and Help & About. Managers also get Visit Import, Reports and Settings. The search button in the top bar searches customers, orders, products and reps; type at least 2 characters.

Customers

  • Search by name, city, phone or ID, and filter by status or type.
  • Tap New Customer, fill in the name, phone, Address Line 1 and City (all required), and tap Use My Location to save the GPS position. Then tap Create Customer.
  • A customer's page shows the last 30 days, recent visits and orders, and the buttons Plan Visit Today and New Order. If Shopify sync shows Draft or Failed, tap Sync to Shopify.
  • A rep can set a customer's location only once. After that, ask a Sales Manager to change it.

A visit, step by step

  1. Open Visits → Today and tap the visit. (Tap New Visit to plan one, then choose the customer, date and purpose and tap Schedule Visit.)
  2. At the shop, tap Check In. The app reads your GPS position. Outside the shop's radius (50 m by default), the check-in still goes through but is marked Outside Geofence.
  3. Tap Take Order, or tap Record No-Order Reason and pick a reason. Add Visit Notes if needed.
  4. Tap Check Out and confirm. A Visit Summary shows the time spent on site.

Check-in opens only on the visit's date, and only one visit can be in progress at a time. On the first visit to a customer with no location, an accurate GPS fix (within 100 m) saves the shop's position.

Taking an order

  1. Choose the customer and the Location to sell from. Set GST % and Discount %; the hint shows your maximum discount.
  2. Add products by searching name, SKU or barcode, or tap the scan button and point the camera at the barcode. If the camera fails, type the code and tap Lookup.
  3. Check the cart. Lines over the available stock show "Exceeds stock — only N available".
  4. Tap Save Draft to finish later, or Submit to send the order. If prices changed in Shopify, tap Apply Updated Prices.

In Orders, filter by All, Drafts, Synced, Pending or Failed. An order's page lets you Submit Order, Retry Shopify Sync, Download Invoice or Resend Email.

Dashboard, commission and profile

  • Dashboard shows sales, orders, completed visits and your Monthly Target progress for Today, This Week, This Month, Last Month or a custom range.
  • Commission shows your Earned, Pending, Reversed and Net Payable amounts, plus your statements.
  • Profile shows your target, maximum discount, commission plan and manager. It also holds Sync Account Data, Log Out and the app-lock switches.

Working with a poor connection

These actions are saved on the phone when you are offline and sent automatically, in order, when the connection returns: new orders (draft or submitted), new visits, check-ins, check-outs and no-order reasons.

  • A banner reads No Internet Connection or Server Unavailable. Tap Retry Connection to try again.
  • An offline check-in shows "Saved offline; the location is verified when it syncs".
  • Orders waiting to send show a Pending badge. If the server rejects one, it shows Failed with the reason.
  • A resent order is never created twice, even after repeated retries.
  • Help & About shows how many items are still waiting.

Do not log out or change the server while items are pending: signing out deletes everything stored on the phone, including the queue.

Push notifications

The mobile app pops up alerts on your phone, even when the app is closed, for the same things that appear in the bell. Shop logins get no push notifications.

You get a push whenWho gets it
A visit is assigned to you or rescheduledThe rep (not when you made the change yourself)
Your order is confirmed in the store (in draft mode, once staff complete the draft)The order's rep
An order fails to sync to the storeThe rep's manager
A shop fails to sync to the storeAdmins
A payment is recorded or receivedThe order's rep and Sales Managers
A commission statement is approved or marked paidThe rep
A product drops to 5 or fewer in stock (once a day per product)Sales Managers

For privacy, a push never shows a shop name, phone number or amount. Tap it to open the record; the full message is in the bell.

On your phone

  1. Sign in to the mobile app. When push is switched on for your site, the app asks to send notifications: tap Allow.
  2. To stop them on this phone, open Profile → Connected Instance & Security and turn off Push notifications. Bell notifications keep working.
  3. Signing out stops pushes to that phone straight away.

Setting it up (admins)

  • On a server where push is already set up, every site has it automatically; there is nothing to do per site.
  • Otherwise, ask your developer to set the Firebase key once for the server. To use a separate key for one site, open Mobile Push Settings in Desk, upload the service account key file, tick Enabled and save.
  • To test, click Send Test Notification in Mobile Push Settings, pick a user under Send to (only users signed in to the app with notifications allowed are listed) and click Send.
  • Without any Firebase setup the app works fully: there are just no pushes, and the app does not ask for notification permission.

Uninstalling and your data

  • Uninstalling Field Sales from your Shopify admin removes its access to your store at once, and syncing stops. Reinstalling reconnects it.
  • 48 hours later Shopify asks us to delete your shop's data: customers' personal data is anonymised, and synced products and log contents are deleted. Order amounts and dates are kept anonymised for your records.
  • Requests from your shoppers to see or delete their data are handled automatically through Shopify.

Can I edit a product's price in Field Sales? No. Products are read-only; change them in Shopify and they sync back.

Does cancelling an order in Field Sales cancel it in Shopify? No. Cancel it in Shopify as well. A paid order must be refunded first.

Does Field Sales take payments? No. Payments go through Shopify's checkout or directly to your business. Mark as Paid records a cash or bank payment in Field Sales; record it in Shopify too with Mark Paid in Shopify.

Can reps work without a signal? Yes, in the mobile app. Orders, visits and check-ins are queued and sent automatically when the connection returns.
